## The supply-model finding (worth reading before the next group call)

With zero genesis outputs and any flat schedule, inflation during year N is 1/(N−1) regardless of the rate: 100% (yr 2), 10% (yr 11), 3% (yr 34). Any target below that curve early requires either a premine (forbidden) or Pirate-style front-loading (the thing the fair-launch rule exists to prevent). A 999,999,999 "genesis supply" would be a premine and is rejected. PLAN.md §8.1 tables P-flat 0.9, an illustrative decay-to-tail, and "restate the constraint" with exact numbers. **This is the one place a genuine human choice remains inside the monetary decision.**
