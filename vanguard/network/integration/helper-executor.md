## Local OS components and their limits

- `CapabilityStore` opens only original pre-reserved operator slots, verifies role/identity/ownership, locks separate open descriptions and uses the existing GrantJournal overwrite/fsync/readback burn. Preparation and first activation use distinct role-bound slots. Separate processes cannot consume one slot twice. A recovered custodian cannot activate even if RESERVED bytes survived. Missing, replaced, corrupt or uncertain state cannot be repaired or recreated by this class. The live secret, role/source binding and independently authenticated custodian remain required. Filesystem/power-loss durability and surviving custody are still unqualified.
- `WriteMeter` durably reserves worst-case cumulative writes before a mediated operation. Failed and partial operations are not refunded. Occupied C/W/A/O capacities, preparation phase/aggregate caps and runtime aggregate writes are checked separately. Persist failure forbids the writer. A recovered balance preserves prior charges. This implements a reservation algorithm, not a filesystem/quota limiter or interception of Docker/kernel/other process writes. A named surviving mediator covering every writer and measured metadata, export and tail cost is still absent. Do not call its counters hard host enforcement.
- `AbsoluteDeadlines` runs in the separately authenticated deadline owner against original monotonic build/work/end times. A late restart executes expired actions without resetting origin. A boot change refuses. Actual independent scheduling, workload StopUnit/KillUnit delivery and survival are unqualified fixtures.
- `ObserverTail` binds the contract and observer PID/start identity, requires independently witnessed exit/reap with status zero, then charges final writes and checks aggregate CPU/memory/PIDs and the original end. It returns SOURCE_TAIL_ACCOUNTED with host cleanup NOT_RUN. Real post-exit sensors, lifecycle survival, reserved tail capacity and every external overhead source remain unqualified.

The executor requires the independent boundary to authenticate source/authority, recheck owned identities and phase order, enforce the absolute deadline and commit hard write admission before each effect. A missing boundary refuses. Its fixture calls are modeled, not an installed root service or actual OS enforcement.
